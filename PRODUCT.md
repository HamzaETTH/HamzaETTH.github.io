# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Hamza E's personal portfolio. They should be able to enjoy and manipulate the particle canvas with a mouse, keyboard, touch, or reduced-motion preference without losing access to the page's identity or controls.

## Product Purpose

Present Hamza E through a compact, interactive particle-network experience. Success means the canvas feels responsive, visually memorable, and technically polished while remaining fast and resilient across modern desktop and mobile browsers.

## Brand Personality

Kinetic, precise, restrained. The experience should feel playful under direct interaction while keeping the existing monochrome, code-oriented portfolio voice.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard or card-grid styling, unrelated portfolio redesigns, decorative UI that competes with the canvas, framework-heavy effects, and motion that ignores user preferences.

## Design Principles

- Make the canvas the identity-bearing surface.
- Reward direct manipulation with immediate, legible feedback.
- Preserve the quiet portfolio shell around ambitious graphics.
- Treat performance and graceful degradation as visible craft.
- Keep advanced controls optional and out of the default view.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard and touch parity, keep controls readable against the dark surface, respect reduced-motion preferences for decorative animation, and maintain functional behavior when WebGL is unavailable.
